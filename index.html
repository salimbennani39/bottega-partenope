The dough

In our hands, tradition. On your table, perfection.

Neapolitan pizza is simple on paper. Dough, San Marzano tomatoes, fior di latte, a very hot oven. The difference is in the hands.

Every base is stretched by hand and baked until the crust puffs up and takes those dark, blistered spots. Soft in the middle, light at the edge, gone in about five minutes.
